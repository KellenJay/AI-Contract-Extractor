    Risk Weight Scale ›

    Every term below carries a risk weight set by the playbook, not the extraction agent. It measures how much an error on that field matters to the underwriting decision — not how hard the field was to find.

    1. 1/5 Informational — wrong or missing has no effect on the decision.
    2. 2/5 Low stakes — a correction is a minor edit, not a re-review.
    3. 3/5 Moderate — affects record accuracy, but isn't a go/no-go factor on its own.
    4. 4/5 High stakes — materially affects pricing, timing, or coverage scope if wrong.
    5. 5/5 Critical — directly drives an accept/decline or a coverage limit; errors carry real financial or compliance exposure.
